PROJECT 04 / BUILT

BarLock.fitness

Fitness coaching and workout tracking platform

BarLock.fitness brings coaches and clients into one training workflow: coaches create and assign programs while clients log workouts, follow progress and turn training data into actionable insights.

Fitness coachingWorkout trackingAnalytics

01 / CORE CAPABILITIES

What it does.

  • Coach and client experiences tailored to each role
  • Training program creation and assignment
  • Structured workout logging
  • Progress and workout analytics
  • Clear visibility into training history

02 / TRAINING WORKFLOW

CONNECTED

How it fits.

  • Coaches organize programs for their clients
  • Clients receive and complete assigned workouts
  • Workout data is logged as training happens
  • Analytics make progress easier to review
  • Both sides stay aligned on the next session

03 / USE CASES

Built for progress.

  • Personal coaches managing client programs
  • Clients following structured training plans
  • Fitness teams reviewing performance over time
